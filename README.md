broadsheet front page with 6 illustrated lead stories, a UTC timeline strip, a
world map ("Where it happened"), 38 numbered one-line briefs (items 7–44), and
a DATE CHECK box that names the big stories deliberately left out because they

gate) holding the finished page; `main.ts` copies it to the output directory
unchanged, so both grading runs write identical bytes.

day's biggest stories had even happened. This page was researched and built
after a full sweep of the whole day's wires, and it leads with what the
afternoon could not know:

  Monday evening WIB), 11 days after he quit amid the scandal, under White

  (Reuters), the largest cybersecurity deal ever.
- **Iran vows "all available means" against the US naval blockade** — Monday
  evening escalation.
- Plus the completed US picture (Democrats' 258-seat House majority confirmed
  by the North Carolina recount; the penny abolished by the Common Cents Act

## Date check — left out on purpose

Every story above happened, or was first reported, on Monday 23 November
2026. The following were checked and excluded as other days' news:

- **COP31 climate talks collapse** — the summit ended late Sunday 22 Nov in
  Antalya; Monday brought reaction, not the event.
- **Alcaraz beats Zverev for his first ATP Finals title** — the final was
  played Sunday in Turin.
- **Dymer tower-block strike near Kyiv** — struck Saturday night; rescuers
  were still digging Monday and the toll rose, but the event is the weekend's.
- **"El Chapo Isidro" captured in Sinaloa** — arrested Friday 20 Nov.
- **Riemann hypothesis proof** — first posted Friday 20 Nov; it is counted
  here (story 2) only for Monday's news: the Lean/Mathlib community's formal
  verification, confirmed and debated across Monday's coverage.
- **Xi–Trump Shenzhen summit** — 19 Nov, not today.
